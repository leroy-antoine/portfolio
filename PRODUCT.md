# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: recruiters and engineers (tech leads, hiring engineers) evaluating a software-engineering or AI internship candidate. A recruiter triages in about 30 seconds (name, role, availability, CV); an engineer then digs into projects (source code, videos, metrics) before proposing an interview.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Antoine Leroy, EPITECH student (GPA 3.04, 80+ projects), seeking a software-engineer or AI-developer internship from early April to end of July. Success: a visitor downloads the CV, opens a project's source, or emails to book an interview.

## Positioning
Low-level systems (C/C++: 42sh, Zappy, Arcade, Raytracer) combined with reinforcement-learning work in Python (Lunar Lander, 95 % success), backed by proof: training videos, crash-rate curves, a won 42 AI hackathon.

## Operating Context
Single-page static site, hosted on any static file host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel). Visited on desktop and mobile, often from a recruiter's link or the CV.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/native JS, no framework, no build step; deployable as-is.
- Content in French; WCAG 2.2 AA; must work without JavaScript; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- Factual content stays exact: figures, distinctions, links and text are not changed or invented.
- Open: Zappy has no repository link; internship year not stated; og:image is a relative URL.

## Evidence on Hand
Real assets in `assets/`: RL training videos (PPO, Q-learning, DQL), crash-rate and training graphs, Zappy demo video, raytracer renders and UML, CV PDF. Awards: 1st place "Agents building Agents" hackathon (42 AI x BlackBox AI, Sept. 2025); EPITECH Code Breaker winner with Corewar and finalist with 42sh (June 2025). No testimonials exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- The work is the proof: real videos, charts and metrics lead over self-description.
- A recruiter must get name, role and availability in seconds.
- Claims stay verifiable and factual.
- Simple, durable, dependency-free delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete, visible focus, 44px targets, reduced-motion support, usable without JavaScript.
